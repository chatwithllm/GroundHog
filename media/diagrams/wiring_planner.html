RTK Rover — Wiring Planner

Robot mower — 3 BLDC motors (2 drive + 1 blade), each with a built-in driver, no MDDS30. Drive: 24V+GND+PWM+DIR+FG. Blade: 24V via relay + e-stop + PWM + FG (independent of drive). Bench-drive with an ESP32; F405/ArduRover for autonomy. Drag tiles; tap a wire to select then drag to reroute; tap a net chip to isolate.

Tap a wire to select + move it. Drag dotted handles to add bends, solid handles to move them, double-tap a bend to remove it. Tap empty space to deselect.
